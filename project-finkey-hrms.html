System Architecture

Engineered a synchronized dual-application HRMS platform (Employee & Admin apps across 50+ modules) that automates daily check-ins, shift logs, leave management, and monthly payroll slips for 500+ active enterprise employees.

Core Engineering & Security Highlights

  • Geofenced GPS Check-In with Fraud Prevention: Engineered precise radius validation coupled with real-time mobile security checks (mock-location spoofing detection, device root, and iOS jailbreak detection) to completely eliminate attendance fraud.
  • Smart Hardware Integrations: Developed automated approval workflows for leave, overtime, and remote-work requests, integrated with corporate office Wi-Fi authentication and smart door access controls.
  • Live Workforce Monitoring: Implemented real-time workforce analytics dashboards, interactive calendar shift scheduling, and automated push notifications for instant shift reassignments.
  • On-Device PDF Generation: Automated high-resolution monthly payslip generation directly on device using Flutter canvas and printing packages.

Production Scale

Successfully rolled out across Google Play Store and Apple App Store with zero biometric sync failures and 99.9% geofence accuracy across diverse Android and iOS hardware configurations.
